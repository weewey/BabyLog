# BabyLog — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 2026_

BabyLog is built to keep your family's data private. In short: **everything you
log stays on your device. We don't run servers, accounts, analytics, ads, or
crash reporting, and we don't share your data with anyone.**

## Data storage

All information you enter — feeds, diapers, growth measurements, milestones,
pumping sessions, medical appointments, photos, and chat messages — is stored
**locally on your device only**, using Apple's SwiftData framework. There is no
cloud backup. Deleting the app permanently deletes all of this data.

## The AI assistant

The chat assistant runs **entirely on your device**. By default it uses **Apple
Intelligence (Apple Foundation Models)**, which is built into iOS and runs
on-device — your messages, questions, and the assistant's replies are never sent
to any server.

You may optionally switch to a different on-device model (Gemma). Selecting it
triggers a **one-time download of the model files from Hugging Face**; that
download is the only network request involved, and it transfers model weights to
your device — **none of your data is uploaded.** After download, that model also
runs entirely on-device.

## Voice input

When you use the microphone to dictate, your speech is transcribed using
**Apple's Speech Recognition**. On supported devices this transcription happens
on-device. We do not store recordings; the resulting text is placed in the chat
composer for you to review and send.

## Photos

Baby photos you attach are stored on your device only and are never uploaded to
any third-party service.

## Calendar

If you grant Calendar access, BabyLog adds your baby's medical appointments to
your own iPhone Calendar so you get reminders. This stays on your device / in
your personal iCloud calendar as you have configured it; BabyLog does not send
your calendar anywhere.

## Third-party services

BabyLog uses **no analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs**.
No data is shared with third parties. Aside from the optional model download
described above, the app makes no network requests with your data.

## Children's privacy

BabyLog records information about an infant on the parent's own device for the
parent's personal use. It does not collect data from children, create accounts,
or transmit any personal information off the device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
